This Privacy Policy explains how TrendScout AI handles information when you use the application.

TrendScout AI is a personal AI-powered product research and content-planning tool designed to help discover product opportunities and analyze trend information.

Depending on the features used, TrendScout AI may access information provided through authorized third-party services, such as Pinterest, solely for the purposes described by the application.

Information is used to support product research, trend analysis, recommendations, and content planning. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

TrendScout AI may interact with third-party services such as Pinterest and product-information providers. Their own privacy policies may apply to information handled by those services.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information accessed by the application. Access credentials and authorization tokens should be kept secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to support the application's intended functionality.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or TrendScout AI, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the project.
